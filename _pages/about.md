## About Me

I am **Gabriela García-Arroyo**, a postdoctoral researcher at the **Instituto de Ciencias Físicas, Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (UNAM)**.

My research focuses on theoretical and observational cosmology, with particular interest in understanding the physical mechanisms governing the evolution of the Universe and the nature of its dark sector.

I investigate cosmological models beyond the standard ΛCDM framework, including dark energy, scalar-field dynamics, neutrino self-interactions, and their implications for cosmic evolution and structure formation.

Beyond research, I am passionate about science communication, astronomy education, and creating opportunities for people to engage with science through creativity and exploration.
## Research Interests

- Theoretical and observational cosmology
- Late dark energy and early dark energy
- Scalar-field cosmological models
- Neutrino physics and self-interactions
- Large-scale structure of the Universe
- Cosmological parameter estimation
- Physics beyond the standard cosmological model

## Science Outreach
I believe that scientific knowledge should be accessible, inspiring, and connected to people's everyday experiences.

I am involved in **Trazos del Cosmos**, a science outreach initiative that brings astronomy and art together to encourage curiosity, creativity, and scientific exploration, particularly among children and young people.

Through interactive workshops, hands-on activities, and artistic expression, we seek to create meaningful encounters with science and inspire new ways of understanding the Universe.
