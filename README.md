I am **Gabriela García-Arroyo**, a researcher interested in cosmology, astrophysics, and understanding the fundamental nature of our Universe.

My academic interests include the evolution of the Universe, its large-scale structure, and the physical processes that shape the cosmos.

Beyond research, I am passionate about **science communication and astronomy education**, particularly creating opportunities for children and young people to explore science through creativity, curiosity, and hands-on experiences.

### Science Outreach

I am involved in **Trazos del Cosmos**, a science outreach initiative that brings astronomy and art together through educational workshops and creative activities.

Through this project, we seek to inspire curiosity about the Universe and encourage girls and young people to see themselves as explorers and creators of scientific knowledge.

### Research Interests

- Cosmology and astrophysics
- Large-scale structure of the Universe
- Astronomy education
- Science communication and public engagement
